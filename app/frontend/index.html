Signed evidence — tamper-evident audit record

Every run is sealed into a hash-chained, Ed25519-signed record. Verify re-checks it three independent ways (content-hash integrity, signature, gate decision-replay). Tamper & re-verify flips one bit in a copy and proves rejection.
